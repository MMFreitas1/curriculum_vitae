APP 01 · MACOS · WINDOWS · ANDROID

AI-ML Refresher

A 15-minute daily reading habit in AI & ML. After noon it gathers what's trending on GitHub, Hugging Face, Hacker News, Reddit and research blogs, ranks it with a fixed formula and keeps the top 15 — on a home-screen widget.

No AI summaries, no API keys: the same inputs always give the same list. Saved items sync across Mac, PC and phone.
