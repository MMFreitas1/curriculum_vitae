APP 01 · MACOS · WINDOWS · ANDROID

AI-ML Refresher

A 15-minute daily reading habit in AI & ML. After noon it gathers what's trending on GitHub, Hugging Face, Hacker News, Reddit and research blogs, ranks it with a fixed formula and keeps the top 15 — on a home-screen widget.

No AI summaries, no API keys: the same inputs always give the same list. Saved items sync across Mac, PC and phone.

APP 02 · MARKETS IN 3D

3D S&P 500 Visualizer

Decades of S&P 500 history as a 3D sector cube and skyline you can scrub through week by week — with sector rotation, warning signs and the market events that moved them, from the dot-com peak onwards.

A built-in analyst assistant reads a stock's fundamentals and the news you pick, over the time frames you choose, and answers short or long.
